In Greek myth, Eris, goddess of strife, was not invited to the wedding of Peleus and Thetis on Mount Pelion. Slighted, she rolled a golden apple inscribed kallisti, for the fairest, into the hall. Hera, Athena, and Aphrodite each claimed it; the dispute that followed became the seed of the Trojan War. To the Romans she was Discordia, sister of Mars and companion of Bellona — across cultures the trickster is rarely a villain alone.
